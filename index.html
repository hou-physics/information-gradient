信息梯度与传播 从直觉到交易 Information Gradient and Propagation From Intuition to Trading

侯政旭 · Zhengxu Hou

作者学物理出身，关注量化投资和基本面投资。

The author trained in physics and follows both quantitative and fundamental investing.

中文版 English edition

交易者觉得价格不对时，这个差距可能来自市场还没反映出来的信息，也可能来自他自己的误读。本书把前一部分叫作信息梯度。全书从一个最简单的根模型出发，每章去掉一两条理想假设，依次讲消息在人群中的传播、人对消息的理解和使用、消息进入价格的过程，最后检验哪些梯度可以拿来交易。每个结论都写明前提、适用范围和依据。

When a trader thinks a price is wrong, the gap may come from information the market has not yet absorbed, or from the trader's own misreading. The book calls the first part the information gradient. Starting from one simple root model and removing one or two idealised assumptions per chapter, it covers how news spreads through people, how people interpret and use it, and how it enters prices, and ends by testing which gradients can be traded. Every result states its premises, its scope and its sources.
